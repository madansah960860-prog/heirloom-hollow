Refund & Return Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

If something is not right, send it back. You have 45 days from the day it is delivered, and the process below takes about five minutes to start.

The return window

You may return an item within 45 days of delivery. The clock starts on the day the carrier records the parcel as delivered, and it is the date you tell us you want to return that counts — not the date the parcel reaches us. Contact us inside the 45 days and you are inside the window, even if the parcel takes another week to come back.

Have a proper look at everything before you commit to it: open the journal and turn every page, stand the print up on the sideboard, smell the candles, deal the cards out. What we cannot take back is a journal that has been written in, a candle that has been lit, or a box that has been engraved — and the reasons for each are below.

Condition we need it in

  • Unused and unwritten-in — reading a journal through is fine, filling in the first page is not.
  • In its original packaging, with the sleeve, slipcase, seals or spare pockets still included.
  • Complete — everything listed under “what is in the box” on the product page.
  • Undamaged by use. Ordinary opening of the box is expected; we do not mind an opened carton.

If an item comes back short of this, we will contact you before doing anything. Where the item is still saleable at a lower value we may offer a partial refund, with the reason and the figure stated, and you can tell us to send it back to you instead at no charge.

Non-returnable items

This list is short and we do not add to it quietly. The following cannot be returned unless they are faulty, damaged in transit, or not what you ordered:

  • Anything engraved or personalised, because we made it to your words and nobody else can use it. We email a proof of the engraving before we cut it, and nothing is engraved until you reply — please read that proof carefully, because it is the last point at which a spelling can be changed.
  • Journals and albums that have been written in, even on one page. Leafing through one is fine; a filled first page is not.
  • Candles that have been lit, for the obvious reason. Unwrap them, smell them, change your mind — just do not put a match to one until you are sure.

Everything else in the shop can be returned. Nothing is excluded because it was reduced, because it was the last one, or because you have returned something before.

How to return something

  1. Tell us. Email hello@heirloomhollow.com with “Return” and your order number in the subject line, or call (207) 555-0155 during Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM ET. Say what you are sending back and why. “Changed my mind” is a complete answer and we will not press you further.
  2. We send you a return number. Within one business day you will get a return authorisation number and the address to send to, in writing.
  3. Pack it. Use the original packaging if you still have it, or any box that protects the item. Write the return number clearly on the outside and put a note with it inside.
  4. Send it to the returns address, which is the same as our mailing address:

    Heirloom Hollow Gifts LLC
    Returns — quote your return number
    88 Harbor Lantern Way, Suite 12
    Portland, ME 04101

    Please use a tracked service. Until it reaches us it is still your parcel, and a tracking number is the only way to prove where it got to.
  5. We inspect and refund. See the timings below.

Damaged, faulty or wrong items

Tell us within 7 days of delivery if a parcel arrives damaged or the contents are broken, so we can make a claim with the carrier while the evidence is fresh. Email hello@heirloomhollow.com with:

  • Your order number.
  • A photograph of the damage.
  • A photograph of the outer box, including the label.

We will send a replacement or refund you in full — your choice — and we pay the return postage. If you would rather keep a slightly marked item at a reduced price, say so and we will agree a figure with you. We will not ask you to deal with the carrier yourself.

If a fault appears later, contact us anyway. Outside the 45 days we are no longer obliged to take it back, but we would rather hear about it than not, and in practice we usually help.

Refunds

Method. We refund to the payment method you used. We do not issue store credit unless you ask for it.

Timing. We inspect returns within 2 business days of the parcel arriving, and issue the refund within 5–10 business days of that inspection. We email you when the refund is sent. Your bank or card issuer may then take a few more days to show it on your statement, which is out of our hands.

Where the Federal Trade Commission’s timings are shorter, they apply. If you paid by cash, cheque or money order, we refund within seven working days of accepting the return. If you paid on a store credit account, we credit it within one billing cycle. If we cancel an order before shipping it, the refund is issued immediately and always within seven working days.

If an engraving comes out wrong

This is not a return and it is not limited by the return window. If the lid does not match the proof you approved, that is our mistake: email hello@heirloomhollow.com with a photograph and we will cut and post a new lid at no charge, however long ago you bought it, and you keep the first one. You do not need to send the box back.

If the proof itself carried the mistake and you approved it, we cannot refund the box — that is the reason we ask you to read the proof carefully. Tell us anyway: we will make a replacement lid at our own cost rather than the shop price, which is usually the cheaper half of the job.

Exchanges

We do not run a formal exchange process, because in practice it is slower for you. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the one you want — that way the new item ships straight away instead of waiting for the old one to arrive. If you would rather we held the new item for you, call and we will.

Cancelling before it ships

If you change your mind before the parcel is handed to the carrier, email hello@heirloomhollow.com or call (207) 555-0155 and we will cancel it and refund you in full. Since we ship within 1–2 business days, please do get in touch quickly.

If a delivery is late or does not arrive

That is handled in the Shipping Policy, which sets out what we do if we cannot meet the shipping time, and how lost parcels are traced. In short: you can always cancel for a full refund rather than wait.
