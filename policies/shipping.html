Shipping Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

We ship your order within 1–2 business days of receiving it. That is a commitment, not an average — and if we ever cannot meet it, the section on delays below explains exactly what we do.

Where we ship

We ship to street addresses in all 50 US states and the District of Columbia. We do not currently ship:

  • Outside the United States.
  • To APO, FPO or DPO military addresses.
  • To US territories including Puerto Rico, Guam and the US Virgin Islands.
  • Everything we sell fits a PO Box except the keepsake box and the framed family tree print, which need a street address.

If your address falls into one of these categories the checkout will not be able to complete. Call (207) 555-0155 and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Processing time — when we ship

We ship your order within 1–2 business days of receiving it. Business days are Monday to Friday and exclude federal holidays. An order placed on a Friday afternoon ships by the following Tuesday at the latest.

Engraved pieces add two business days. We email a proof of the exact text, we wait for your reply, and only then does the laser run — so an engraved order ships within four business days of your approving the proof, plus however long you take over it. Please do take your time over it; that email is the last point at which a spelling can be fixed.

You will get an email with a tracking number when the parcel is handed to the carrier. If you have not had that email by the end of the second business day — the fourth for an engraved piece — contact us, because something has gone wrong and we would like to know.

Carriers

We ship by USPS and UPS. We choose the carrier based on the size and weight of the parcel and the destination; you cannot currently select one. Most parcels do not need a signature. The carrier decides whether to leave a parcel unattended, and we cannot override that decision from here.

Delivery delays and your right to cancel

The Federal Trade Commission’s Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule governs what happens when a seller cannot ship on time. Here is exactly what we do.

  1. We tell you before the deadline passes. If we realise we cannot ship within 1–2 business days, we contact you before that period expires — not afterwards.
  2. We give you a definite new shipping date, or say plainly that we cannot give one.
  3. We offer you the choice. Accept the new date, or cancel for a full refund. The refund includes any shipping you paid.
  4. If the new date is more than 30 days later, or we cannot give a date at all, we need your explicit agreement to continue. If you do not reply, we cancel the order and refund you automatically. We will not sit on your money waiting for stock.
  5. Refunds for cancelled orders are issued immediately, and always within seven working days.

You can cancel a delayed order at any point before it ships simply by emailing hello@heirloomhollow.com or calling (207) 555-0155. You do not need a reason.

Tracking your order

Every parcel ships with tracking, and the number is in your shipping email. If tracking has not updated for three business days, contact us and we will open a trace with the carrier on your behalf. You do not have to call the carrier yourself.

Lost packages

If tracking shows no movement for seven business days, or shows “delivered” but nothing arrived, tell us. We ask you to check with anyone else at the address and look in the usual places first, because most of these turn up within a day.

After that we open a formal trace with the carrier. Traces usually take five to eight business days. We do not make you wait for the outcome — once the trace is open we send a replacement or refund you in full, whichever you prefer, and we deal with the carrier ourselves.

Damaged packages

If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, photograph it before opening if you can. Email hello@heirloomhollow.com within seven days of delivery with a photograph of the damage and of the outer box including the label. We will send a replacement or a full refund, and we pay the return postage. The full process is in the Refund & Return Policy.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses

Please check the address at checkout — a missing apartment number is the most common cause of a failed delivery. If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable because of an address error, we will contact you and re-send it once you confirm the correct address; we will ask you to cover the second postage. If you would rather cancel at that point, we refund the item price in full.

If you spot a mistake in your address, call (207) 555-0155 straight away. If the parcel has not left us we will simply correct it at no cost.

Multiple items

We ship an order in one parcel wherever it fits. The tabletop radio and the digital photo frame are bulky enough that they sometimes travel separately from the books, and an engraved box waits for its proof and so nearly always travels on its own. If we split an order you will get a tracking number for each parcel, and you are never charged twice for shipping.
