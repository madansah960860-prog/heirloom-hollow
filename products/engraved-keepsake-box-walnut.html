Engraved Keepsake Box, Walnut

$68.00

SKU HHG-604 · In stock

A solid walnut box with a brass-hinged lid that stays where you put it, and a felt-lined interior deep enough for letters, photographs or a christening cup. Up to three lines are laser-engraved on the lid — we email you a proof and cut nothing until you reply. The finish is hard-wax oil, so a mark can be rubbed out rather than being permanent.

Before you order: Engraved items cannot be returned unless they are faulty, because we make them to your words. We email a proof of the exact text before anything is cut and wait for your reply — please read it carefully, as that is the last point at which a spelling can be corrected.

Shipping: We ship your order within 1–2 business days of receiving it. Standard shipping is $5.95, free on orders over $55, and arrives in 4–6 business days after it ships.

Returns: Return anything unused in its original packaging within 45 days of delivery. We refund to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of inspecting the return. Read the full policy.

Sales tax is calculated at checkout from your delivery address. There are no handling fees or surcharges.

What it is

  • Solid walnut, not veneer
  • Up to three engraved lines, 28 characters each
  • We email a proof and engrave nothing until you approve it
  • Brass quadrant hinge — the lid stays open
  • Felt-lined, 3 in deep inside
  • Hard-wax oil finish; marks can be rubbed out

In the box

  • Keepsake box
  • Care card
  • A cloth for the oil finish
